Frequently asked questions

Before you start.

Does FeeVeto find or cancel subscriptions automatically?

No. You enter subscriptions yourself. FeeVeto does not access bank accounts, manage provider accounts, or cancel services.

How are recommendations made?

A deterministic set of documented rules weighs usage, importance, satisfaction, household use, overlap, contracts, seasonality, and switching difficulty. Every result includes reasons and a confidence level.

Does changing currency convert prices?

No. The display currency updates examples, new-entry defaults, the matching dashboard subtotal, and the default market for alternatives. An entered country overrides that market. Every saved subscription and verified provider price keeps its original currency. FeeVeto shows separate original-currency subtotals and never combines currencies because it has no live exchange-rate service.

Where is my information stored?

Your original subscription list and current journey draft stay in this browser. A personal assessment is calculated on FeeVeto’s backend. If you choose Save this audit, its request, answers, comparison, and dated history are stored in your account on Cloudflare. Signing in alone does not upload your local list. Local JSON exports do not include account history.

What does creating an account do?

Clerk provides sign-in and profile management. You can then choose to save individual personal assessments and reopen their dated history. Signing in alone does not upload your browser subscription list or grant Premium. FeeVeto checks account permissions before showing restricted comparisons.

Is FeeVeto Premium a subscription?

No. FeeVeto Premium is one lifetime purchase for the account used at checkout. Stripe handles the payment; FeeVeto stores only the server-side references needed to verify access. Owner and approved beta accounts keep complimentary premium access without payment.

Are alternative prices always current?

Only alternatives with a verification date and sufficient local data are displayed. You should still confirm prices, features, and availability with the provider.
