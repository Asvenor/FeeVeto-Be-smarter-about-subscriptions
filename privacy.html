Plain-language overview

Privacy

Your subscription entries are stored locally in this browser and are not attached to your Clerk account. Clearing browser data, using private-browsing mode, or switching devices may remove or hide your saved entries.

Information stored in this browser

Names, prices, original billing currencies, the global display-currency preference, renewal dates, categories, usage and importance choices, unified-form answers, statuses, and provider links you enter are saved using local storage. FeeVeto has no cloud database or synchronization for your audit in this release.

The display currency changes illustrative examples, defaults for new entries, and the default market used for alternatives. An entered country overrides that market. It does not convert or relabel saved subscription or provider prices.

Optional accounts

FeeVeto uses Clerk for optional authentication and profile management. If you sign up or sign in, Clerk processes the identity information and authentication method you provide. FeeVeto may store a server-only role and complimentary beta-access setting in Clerk private metadata. These settings control access only and are not subscription-audit data. FeeVeto does not copy your subscription audit into Clerk. Review Clerk’s Privacy Policy for details about its processing.

Payments and premium access

If you choose FeeVeto Premium, Stripe processes the checkout, payment method, billing details, and payment confirmation. FeeVeto does not receive or store your complete card number. Its Cloudflare database stores your Clerk user ID together with Stripe customer, checkout, and payment references, the purchased product and price identifiers, amount, currency, entitlement status, and timestamps so the backend can grant or revoke access. This payment record is separate from your browser-only subscription audit. Review Stripe’s Privacy Policy for details about its processing.

Alternative matching

When you save a supported service, FeeVeto sends only the selected service identifier, product type, structured must-have, nice-to-have, and not-needed requirements, an optional country code, the selected display currency as a market hint, required platform, applicable title, game, VPN server country, target language, learning level or course subject, free or paid alternative choices, advertisement and free-limit preferences, and required storage amount to its Cloudflare backend. It does not send the subscription name, entered price, private notes, calculated totals, or your full subscription list. If you are signed in, the request includes your Clerk session so the backend can determine whether you may receive the complete catalogue.

Backups and deletion

Exporting creates a JSON backup on your device. Importing reads the file you choose in your browser. You can delete individual entries, clear the audit, or remove this site’s data through browser settings.

Analytics

FeeVeto does not currently use product analytics. Subscription names, entered prices, questionnaire answers, and calculated totals are not sent to an analytics service.

Website hosting

FeeVeto’s production site and protected API use Cloudflare hosting. Cloudflare processes ordinary network request information needed to operate and secure the service. Review the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. The source repository may also publish a static GitHub Pages preview.

External links

Provider and alternative links open third-party websites. Feedback opens Google Forms. Information submitted there is governed by the destination service and form owner. Review Google’s Privacy Policy before submitting feedback.

Independent tool

FeeVeto is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any subscription provider. Always verify prices, features, renewal dates, and cancellation terms directly.
